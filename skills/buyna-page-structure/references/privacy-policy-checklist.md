# Privacy Policy Planning Checklist

Include a Privacy Policy page and a footer link by default when the website collects, receives, stores, or processes personal information.

Before any final policy text is drafted, collect or mark `待补充`:

- Operating company or responsible entity.
- Business address and contact method.
- Applicable country or jurisdiction.
- Personal information collected through forms, accounts, orders, bookings, analytics, cookies, or other features.
- Purpose of collection and use.
- External services and processors, including hosting, analytics, email, payment, maps, and embedded media.
- Retention, deletion, access, correction, and inquiry procedures.
- Cookie or tracking choices when applicable.
- Effective date and update method.

Skill 3 plans the page, link, and required inputs. It must not invent legal terms, guarantee compliance, or replace qualified legal review.
