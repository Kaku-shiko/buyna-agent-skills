# Website Structure Method

## Full Planning Mode

When a supplied screenshot or full-page image is a structure reference, start from the approved numbered audit. Verify its visible section count, order, component composition, and represented information before adapting it. Keep an explicit mapping from every reference section to the planned page section; label intentional merge, split, removal, or addition decisions.

Start with the smallest useful structure:

- Header: logo, primary navigation, primary action, optional language/login controls, and mobile menu.
- Main: page-specific content sections.
- Footer: company summary, navigation, contact information, address when approved, policy links, and copyright.

For a typical company homepage, consider this order and remove anything the customer does not need:

1. Hero.
2. Services or products.
3. Company introduction.
4. Advantages or proof.
5. Cases, testimonials, or partners when real material exists.
6. FAQ when useful.
7. Contact or final call to action.

Create additional pages only when their content cannot be communicated clearly on the homepage, such as About, Services, Products, Cases, News, FAQ, Contact, Privacy Policy, or account-related pages.

Also plan these website-wide requirements:

- One clear primary action and its destination.
- Contact and form success/error behavior.
- 404 page and useful route back to the site.
- Loading, empty, validation, permission, and failure states for dynamic features.
- Page title, meta description, social-share image, favicon, and language information.
- Real content sources for company information, services, products, categories, prices, testimonials, partners, and legal links. Mark dynamic product, category, stock, image, availability, and price sections as backend-managed rather than hard-coded.
- Language switch and translated content when the site is multilingual.
- Seller/admin or account entry only when the approved website requires it.

## Template Review Mode

Do not repeat the full design process. Inspect the selected template and record:

- Keep: pages and sections that match the customer.
- Remove: irrelevant or unsupported pages and sections.
- Add: missing required pages or sections.
- Replace: template copy, images, branding, links, forms, and data that require customer material.
