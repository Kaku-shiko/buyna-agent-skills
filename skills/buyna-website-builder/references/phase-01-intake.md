# Phase 1: Customer Information

Require website type, primary/additional languages, company/brand name and address, public price display, currency, and available logo/copy/product/service/media/reference materials. Use supplied product prices when present; otherwise set the price source to `merchant_dashboard` without asking. When no Logo or specific images are supplied, automatically mark each as `之后补全`; do not ask or block the gate, and do not treat generated or placeholder assets as approved customer materials. Present one customer-information record for approval.
